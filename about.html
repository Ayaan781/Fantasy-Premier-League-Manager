ABOUT FPL SIDEKICK

Good advice starts with good questions.

We’re building a calmer way to think through Fantasy Premier League decisions: grounded in the numbers, explained in plain English, and finished with a human point of view.

Placeholder copy: the team story and background below are fictional concept text for the first design pass. They are not claims about a real company or named analysts.

THE IDEA

Less noise. Better context.

FPL has a lot of numbers, but a number by itself rarely tells the whole story. Sidekick puts form, price, availability and fixture difficulty together so managers can see the trade-offs behind a pick—then add their own judgement before making a move.
