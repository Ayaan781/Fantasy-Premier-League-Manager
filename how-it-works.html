THE REVIEW METHOD

Show the factors. Keep the call yours.

Submit a valid 15-player squad to get a transparent first-pass report. I’ll review your squad and send personal feedback from Gmail to the email you provide.

The site uses a saved FPL data snapshot. It is a decision aid—not a points guarantee, official FPL feature or substitute for checking news before the deadline.

THE CHECKS

What the first pass looks at

01 · VALIDATE

Confirm the squad rules

Checks for 15 unique players, 2 goalkeepers, 5 defenders, 5 midfielders, 3 forwards, the £100m budget and a maximum of three players from one club.

02 · PLAYER TRENDS

Read recent and season output

Shows form, next-gameweek expected points, season points, minutes, goals, assists, clean sheets and availability notes for every squad member.

03 · FIXTURE RUN

Compare the next five gameweeks

Displays each player’s upcoming home/away opponents and official FPL fixture difficulty. The starting XI’s average difficulty gives a quick outlook.

FROM SUBMISSION TO REPLY

A direct request, then a human review.

When you submit your squad, the page shows an instant score out of 100 alongside player-by-player notes, then sends the report to my Formspree review inbox. The score weighs same-position expected-points and form percentiles, fixture ease and availability. The starting XI contributes 75% and the bench 25%. I’ll review the submission and email personal feedback from Gmail. Nothing on this site opens the manager’s email app.

THE MANAGER

Build and submit

Pick the XI and bench, choose a formation, and provide an email address. The squad and first-pass review go to the configured inbox through Formspree.

PERSONAL FEEDBACK

A human reply from Gmail

I review each squad and send personal feedback to the email address entered with the submission.

INBOX DELIVERY

Confirm your recipient

Formspree routes submissions to the recipient configured for the form. Confirm that it is your review inbox before accepting live submissions.
